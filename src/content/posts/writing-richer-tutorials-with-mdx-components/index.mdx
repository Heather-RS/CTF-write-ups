THE DISAPPEARANCE OF OWAIN HUGHES
This is a test or beginners CTF to see if you can gather intel from multiple sources and find evidence

The Breif 
The Hughes family is deeply concerned. Their 19-year-old son, Owain, has abruptly left their home in Llandudno. While he had been mentioning a girlfriend in South Wales, his sudden silence and isolation have left his parents worried about his well-being.

As a Private Investigator, your first task is to review the initial evidence. We need to confirm the core facts from the family's briefing and the recovered photos before we begin tracking his journey south.

Questions one:

Review the attached PDF and images to confirm the missing individual's full name, year of birth, and the city where he usually lives.
