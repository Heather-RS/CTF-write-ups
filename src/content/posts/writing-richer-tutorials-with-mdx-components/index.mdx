THE DISAPPEARANCE OF OWAIN HUGHES

This is a test or beginners CTF to see if you can gather intel from multiple sources and find evidence

The Breif 
The Hughes family is deeply concerned. Their 19-year-old son, Owain, has abruptly left their home in Llandudno. While he had been mentioning a girlfriend in South Wales, his sudden silence and isolation have left his parents worried about his well-being.

As a Private Investigator, your first task is to review the initial evidence. We need to confirm the core facts from the family's briefing and the recovered photos before we begin tracking his journey south.

<Callout type="Question One">

Review the attached PDF and images to confirm the missing individual's full name, year of birth, and the city where he usually lives.

Easy enough just search the PDF for answers

A bittersweet memory 
The photo features a pier with a simple written note 07/12/2024 "our favourite spot, why did Kelly have to go? :("

-So first thought is, who is Kelly, unfortunately there is nothing to suggest Kelly is known to Owain as the Family is equally confused?
-So then what else could it be? Well first we need to look at the image, we see a tourist attaction called a talking telescope. A quick reverse image serch finds the exact type of telescope, with the same fence style.
-The location Llandudno Pier, ok so that matched his last known location, so what about Kelly?
-Well now we type into the search bar, Llandudno Pier Kelly. 
-Kelly's ice cream kiosks on piers are active at locations like the Claremont Pier in Lowestoft, though the famous unit on the Llandudno Pier was destroyed by severe weather.
-Ok so its a Kisok for ice-cream, when did this happen? The Kelly's Ice Cream kiosk on Llandudno Pier was destroyed and washed away during Storm Darragh on December 7, 2024
-OK so it matches up, to the date on the photo. Bingo we have an answer. Second flag is in
